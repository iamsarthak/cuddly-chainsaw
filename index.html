Frequently Asked Questions

What is a WhatsApp chat link?

A WhatsApp chat link is a direct URL that opens a WhatsApp conversation with a specific phone number without the need to save the contact first. These links can include pre-written messages that appear in the chat box automatically.

Do users need WhatsApp installed to use these links?

Yes, the person clicking the link needs to have the corresponding app (WhatsApp, Telegram, or Signal) installed on their device. The link will open the app directly to your chat.

Does DirectChatNow store my phone number or messages?

No, DirectChatNow doesn't store any of your data. All link and QR code generation happens locally in your browser. Your phone number and messages are not saved on our servers.

Can I customize the appearance of the QR codes?

Currently, we offer standard QR code generation. In the future, we plan to add customization options including colors, logos, and design elements to match your brand identity.

Are there any limits to how many links I can create?

No, you can create as many chat links and QR codes as you need, completely free of charge. There are no limits or hidden fees.

How can I share the QR code with others?

You can right-click on the generated QR code and save it as an image. From there, you can print it, add it to your website, include it in emails, or print it on marketing materials like business cards, flyers, or product packaging.
